Three schools, each carrying the mission forward

From a secondary and vocational campus in Mbale City to nursery and primary schools in Wampewo and the Kween hills — every Green Pasture school is free to visit, and each has (or will soon have) its own dedicated site.

Secondary & Vocational · est. 2001
Musoto, Nabitiri Parish · Mbale City

Green Pasture Secondary & Vocational School

Uganda's first free secondary school in Mbale, opened in 2001 to give youth who had dropped out for lack of school fees a second chance. Students combine academic study with over 20 vocational skill areas, supported by 10+ industry partnerships and a 95% graduate-to-employment or further-education rate.

Visit school site greenpasturesecondary.sc.ug
Wampewo · Mbale District

Wampewo Green Pasture Nursery & Primary School

A milestone for a community once disconnected from the rest of Mbale by swampland. Since opening, school drop-out rates in the area have fallen, and the school now serves over 470 nursery and primary pupils.

Trio Hill View Nursery & Primary School

Our newest campus, in the hills of Ngenge, Kween District — a village that readily welcomed Green Pasture to work alongside it. The school now serves around 450 pupils.
