What can the poor do to help the poor?

That single question, asked in the slums of Maluku in 1998, became a ministry that today runs three schools, a growing college, and a wider mission of empowerment across Eastern Uganda.

Our Journey

From a dream to three campuses

June 1998

A name given in a dream

Born from a meeting between a struggling pastor of Redeemed Church Maluku and Philemon Barasa, a recent S.6 leaver, Green Pasture Christian Outreach was conceived to answer one question: what can the poor do to help the poor, and together lead a better life? During a season of prayer and fasting, Pastor Grace Buluma received a dream that gave the ministry its name — drawn from Psalm 23:1–2.

2001

Green Pasture Secondary & Vocational School opens

The first free secondary education in Mbale, giving youth who had dropped out for lack of school fees a second chance at both academic and vocational learning, right within their own community.

Growth

Wampewo Green Pasture Primary School

The first school of its kind in a Wampewo community once cut off from the rest of Mbale by swampland. School drop-out rates have fallen since, and the school now serves over 470 pupils.

Newest

Trio Hill View Nursery & Primary School

Established in Ngenge Sub-County, Kween District, and welcomed warmly by the local community. The school now serves around 450 pupils.

Today & Beyond

Green Pasture College — and a University still to come

Currently offering Diploma and Certificate courses in Theology, with Business Administration, Public Administration and Computer Studies awaiting NCHE approval — one step on the road to the long-held vision of a full Green Pasture University.

From the Director

Looking Ahead

Honest about the road still ahead

Like most grassroots ministries, GPCO works with real constraints — inadequate funding for administration, a small volunteer-heavy team, and growing numbers of orphans the organisation wishes it could support more fully. We are also working deliberately to strengthen strategic planning and make better use of technology, of which this website is one small step.

Our future plans are clear: expand our programmes to new areas of Uganda, grow our land to around 20 acres to support the college's development, and keep working toward the vision of a full Green Pasture University.
