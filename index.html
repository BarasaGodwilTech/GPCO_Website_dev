Green Pasture Secondary & Vocational School

Uganda's first free secondary school in Mbale, opened in 2001. Academic education paired with hands-on vocational training in trades that lead straight to work.

June, 1998 · Maluku, Mbale

A name given in a dream

"What are you doing?" Pastor Grace Buluma asked in the dream. The answer came without hesitation: "I'm digging the foundation of green pasture."

It was born from a single question a struggling pastor and a young school-leaver kept asking each other in the slums of Maluku — what can the poor do to help the poor, and together lead a better life?

Beyond the Classroom

A wider ministry of empowerment

Schools are only one part of the work. GPCO also equips Christian leaders, strengthens families and grows local enterprise across Eastern Uganda.

01

Equipping Conference for Leaders

An annual holistic training for pastors and Christian leaders — around 600 attend each year, over 1,100 to date — covering spiritual, social and economic growth.

02

Family & Marriage Support

A trained team of women walk alongside other women through marriage and family challenges, then go on to train others in turn.

03

Green Pasture SACCO

A savings and credit cooperative that has helped grow over 40 small businesses in the community, building a culture of saving rather than dependence.

04

Christian Chamber of Commerce

Formed with fellow stakeholders to connect Christian business people, sharpen business skills, and grow local and international trade links.

05

Green Pasture College

Currently offering Diploma and Certificate courses in Theology, with Business Administration, Public Administration and Computer Studies awaiting NCHE approval.

06

Green Pasture University Vision 2030

The long-held goal since the secondary school's launch — a world-class Christian university serving Eastern Uganda and beyond.
