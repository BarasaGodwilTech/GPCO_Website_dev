Our Programmes

Six ways GPCO empowers the community

01 · Leadership

Equipping Conference for Leaders

A holistic annual training that has shaped hundreds of pastors and Christian leaders, teaching biblical principles alongside practical social and economic growth. About 600 attend each year, with over 1,100 leaders trained across the ministry's history.

02 · Family

Family & Marriage Support

A team of trained women support fellow women through marriage-related challenges, and go on to train others — multiplying support through the community rather than centralising it.

03 · Enterprise

Green Pasture SACCO

A savings and credit cooperative encouraging a culture of saving and responsible credit. Over 40 small-scale businesses have grown through its support, turning borrowers into established local business people.

04 · Enterprise

Christian Chamber of Commerce

Formed alongside other stakeholders to bring Christian business people together — sharpening business skills, growing capital, and opening both local and international trade connections.

05 · Education

Green Pasture College

Currently offering Diploma and Certificate courses in Theology. Business Administration, Public Administration and Computer Studies await NCHE approval, having already been recommended as viable.

06 · Vision

Green Pasture University

Projected at the launch of the secondary school and still the ministry's ultimate educational goal — a full, world-class Christian university serving Eastern Uganda.In progress

Working Together

Partnerships that carry the work further

GPCO works alongside local and international partners including Equipping Leaders International, Oasis Uganda, Goshen Blessings Uganda and Fields of Life. Over 80 churches across the eastern part of the country have partnered in leadership training, birthing new church plants and connections as far as the USA, UK, Kenya and Nigeria.

These partnerships include fellowship with Pentecostal churches in Uganda, the United Methodist Church USA, Anglican and Presbyterian churches, and Evangelical churches of West Africa — a shared conviction, in the words of John 17, "that they all may be one."
